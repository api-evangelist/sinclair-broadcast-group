SAVANNAH, Ga. — July 27, 2026 — Sinclair today announced the appointment of Sierra Horton as Vice President and General Manager of WTGS (FOX) in Savannah and WGXA (FOX/ABC) in Macon, Georgia. A Savannah native, Horton joined Sinclair in 2017 and has advanced through a series of leadership roles supporting both WTGS and WGXA.
