BIRMINGHAM, Ala. – September 3, 2026 – Sinclair today announced that WTTO, its Birmingham station has rebranded as The 205. The new name is inspired by Birmingham’s 205 area code and reflects WTTO’s continued commitment to serving the Birmingham community with programming that is relevant, local and connected to the market.
