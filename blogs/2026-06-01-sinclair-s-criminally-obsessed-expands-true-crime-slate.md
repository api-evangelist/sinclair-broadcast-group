Film explores the murder of Texas media executive Steven Beard and the conviction of his troubled wife, Celeste, through exclusive interviews and firsthand accounts Hunt Valley, MD — June 1, 2026 — Sinclair today announced the premiere of a new original documentary from its Criminally Obsessed crime franchise, Celeste: Trophy Wife or Framed for Life , a gripping examination of one of Texas’s most sensational murder cases involving wealth, power, betrayal, and a conviction that continues to spark debate decades later. Set against the backdrop of Austin’s elite social scene in the late 90’s and early 00’s, Celeste: Trophy Wife or Framed for Life revisits the murder of wealthy media executive Steven Beard and the subsequent conviction of his wife Celeste Beard. The documentary will premiere Friday, June 5 on the Criminally Obsessed YouTube channel.
