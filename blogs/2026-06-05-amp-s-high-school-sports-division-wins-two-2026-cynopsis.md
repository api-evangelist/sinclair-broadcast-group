Best High School Sports Innovation and Marketing Executive of the Year Hunt Valley, MD — June 5, 2026 — AMP Sports, a division of Sinclair, today announced that its High School Division has been honored with two 2026 National Cynopsis Sports Awards, Best High School Sports Innovation and Marketing Executive of the Year. The recognition highlights Sinclair's continued leadership in expanding access to high school sports and driving meaningful community impact. In the Best High School Sports Innovation category, AMP was recognized for redefining how audiences access high school athletics.
