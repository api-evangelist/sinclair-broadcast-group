12 of the Company’s Content Centers Honored for Outstanding Journalism WJAR/ Providence, RI Honored for Overall Excellence KATV/Little Rock, AR and WRGB/Albany, NY Named Best Newscasts Additional Honors include Breaking News, Hard News, News Series, Feature Reporting, Continuing Coverage Investigative Reporting, Sports Reporting, Digital and Podcast Baltimore, MD – May 28, 2026 –Sinclair is proud to announce 12 of its owned and/or operated content centers across the country have been honored with a total of 21 RTDNA Regional Edward R. Murrow Awards for Outstanding Journalism, a testament to the company’s commitment to providing the best in local journalism. The Murrow Awards are sponsored by the Radio Television Digital News Association ( RTDNA ) and honor outstanding achievements in broadcast and digital journalism.
