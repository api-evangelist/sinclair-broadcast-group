ALBANY, N.Y. — July 29, 2026 — Sinclair today announced the appointment of Michael Beadnell as Vice President and General Manager of WRGB/CBS6 and WCWN/CW15 in Albany, New York. An Albany native, Beadnell has spent more than 25 years in the broadcast industry serving New York's Capital Region.
