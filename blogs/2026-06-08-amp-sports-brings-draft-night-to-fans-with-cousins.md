NEW YORK — June 8, 2026- AMP Sports, a division of Sinclair, today announced Cousins Courtside , a Draft night experience with NBA legends Vince Carter and Tracy McGrady, co-hosts of the hit podcast Cousins . The live event will take place in New York City on Tuesday, June 23. Carter and McGrady will bring fans up close for one of basketball's biggest nights with a 30-minute Cousins live show, followed by a Draft watch party as the two hosts provide live commentary and a Q&A during the live broadcast.
